# OCR dependencies

These unmodified assets were obtained from the npm registry. Versions and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in manifest.json.

- Tesseract.js 6.0.1: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js — Apache-2.0.
- Tesseract.js-core 6.0.0: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core — Apache-2.0. The two LSTM wasm.js builds include their WebAssembly data.
- Japanese and English trained data: @tesseract.js-data/jpn and @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0, 4.0.0_best_int variant, from https://github.com/naptha/tessdata. The npm packages declare MIT; underlying Tesseract training data originates from the Apache-2.0 Tesseract project (https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best).

The Apache-2.0 license is included as LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt. Bundled JavaScript dependency notices are retained in the accompanying *.LICENSE.txt files.

The model and library files are served locally by this site. They do not require a third-party OCR service.
